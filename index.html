FAQ

Questions we get every day

How fast is setup, really?

About 60 seconds. The panel creates your server as soon as payment clears, and you get the IP right away. Large modpacks add a few minutes for the first install.

Which versions and modpacks do you support?

Vanilla, Paper, Purpur, Fabric, and Forge, from 1.8.8 to the latest release. One-click installs cover CurseForge and Modrinth packs, and you can upload your own jars over FTP.

How do backups work?

We snapshot your whole server every night and keep 7 days of history. Restores are one click in the panel, and you can download any backup as a zip before you roll back.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Any time. Upgrades apply in about a minute, and your world, plugins, and settings carry over. We prorate what you already paid, so you only cover the difference.

What is your refund policy?

Full refund within 72 hours of your first order, no questions asked. After that, you can cancel any time and keep the server until the paid period ends.

What does DDoS protection actually cover?

Every plan includes always-on filtering. Volumetric attacks are absorbed at our edge, and game-protocol floods are rate-limited before they reach your node. It costs nothing extra.
